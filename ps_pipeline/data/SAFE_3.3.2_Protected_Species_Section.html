3.3.2.1 Data aggregation and key findings

This chapter aggregates the results of the three summary tables (Tables 2, 2b, 2c), the Bayesian anomaly check, the incidental take statement (ITS) tracker and the multi-disciplinary context reviewed by the working group, to characterise protected-species interactions in the 2025 fishing year. Anomalies are judged with an OR gate (OR gate: robust_anomaly >= 2 OR percentile >= 90 OR Bayesian tail prob < alpha).

Key findings

  • 1 of 6 monitored species is flagged High in 2025: Loggerhead turtle (39 vs median 16; gates: percentile, bayes).
  • 5 of 6 species are above their historical median and 6 show an increasing long-run slope; the interaction-rate anomaly agrees in sign with the count anomaly for 6 of 6, so most of the change is not an artefact of effort.
  • The current count falls outside the Gamma-Poisson posterior predictive 90% interval for Oceanic whitetip shark (below), Loggerhead turtle (above); every other current count sits inside its interval.
  • Interaction centroids moved more than 250 km for Oceanic whitetip shark (808 km), Shortfin mako shark (348 km), Laysan albatross (297 km); 12.5% of leatherback turtle interactions fell outside the historical 90% area. Spatial redistribution, not abundance, is the leading explanation where the rate anomaly is modest.
  • Fleet selection anomalies (interaction-set environment minus fleet-wide environment) are at historical extremes for Chlorophyll-a (2 species), Distance to seamount (1 species), SST (4 species), Sea-level anomaly (1 species), indicating the fleet fished a different habitat envelope than usual rather than the ocean alone changing.
  • Fleet effort share changed most in the Latitude: south of 20°N stratum (+17.6 points; Low bigeye CPUE north; targeting yellowfin).
  • No ITS running-sum alert; Loggerhead turtle stands at 84% of its window limit after 5 of 5 years.
  • Causal diagnosis for Loggerhead turtle cannot be closed from the data alone; it needs expert input on gear/method change and year-class strength.

Methods and provenance

Robust anomaly = (current − median) ÷ (1.4826 × MAD) over historical years. Percentile = share of historical years strictly below the current value. Trend = log-linear Poisson GLM with observed sets as offset, upgraded to negative binomial when Pearson dispersion exceeds 1.5. Bayesian check = Gamma-Poisson conjugate model with a method-of-moments prior; P(X ≥ current) evaluated by the negative-binomial–incomplete-beta identity. Flag = OR gate over the three tests.
